# Lesson Plan 2 — Work and Wellbeing

## Focus
Students investigate how industrial change can shape financial security, identity, community wellbeing and social connection.

## Learning intentions
- explain how work affects wellbeing
- compare positive and negative changes in a community
- use evidence to support opinions about industrial change

## Key activities
- workshop closure scenario
- research source analysis
- opinion line discussion

## Evidence of learning
- student responses to evidence-informed discussion
- reflective notes
- exit ticket summary
