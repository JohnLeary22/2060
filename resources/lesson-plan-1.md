# Lesson Plan 1 — Types of Work

## Focus
Students identify the range of jobs and work arrangements linked to different industries and local change.

## Learning intentions
- explain how work can change over time in a place
- compare different work arrangements and occupations
- connect local industry changes to employment patterns

## Key activities
- Industry workforce challenge
- Worker sort activity
- Industry collision

## Evidence of learning
- student reasoning during sorting tasks
- exit ticket response
- quick quiz responses
